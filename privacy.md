# Privacy Policy for `Local Darts Tracker`

This Privacy Policy describes how `Local Darts Tracker` (short, the "App") handles your information. This App is designed to operate entirely offline and does not collect, transmit, or share any of your personal data.

## 1. Information Collection and Use

The App only processes information that you manually enter, specifically:

    Player Profiles: Names or nicknames of players.

    Game Data: Scores, statistics, and match history.

This data is used solely to provide the core functionality of the App (tracking your darts games) and is never sent to any external servers.

## 2. Local Storage

All data entered into the App is stored locally and on your device only.

## 3. Data Sharing

Since the App does not connect to the internet, your data is never shared with third parties, advertisers, or any external entities.

## 4. Data Retention and Deletion

You are in full control of your data.

    You can edit or delete player profiles and related score data within the App at any time.

    If you uninstall the App, all data stored within it will be permanently deleted by your device's operating system.

## 5. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last Updated" date at the bottom of this page.
